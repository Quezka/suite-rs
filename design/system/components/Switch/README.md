# Switch

The on/off control for every yes-or-no setting; never a checkbox in Settings.

Sits at the right of a setting row; the row's title says what it turns on. The knob moves in 180ms.

The consumer supplies the words (translated) and what happens on press. In code: `quezka_kit::widgets` (Rust), `quezka_ui.widgets` / `settings` (Python), `ui/` (Kotlin).
