# SettingsCard

A card of settings rows: a title and a wrapping hint on the left, the control on the right.

Group related settings in one card with an `accent` caption heading. Hints wrap; nothing is cut off. Cards are `gap` apart.

The consumer supplies the words (translated) and what happens on press. In code: `quezka_kit::widgets` (Rust), `quezka_ui.widgets` / `settings` (Python), `ui/` (Kotlin).
